## Purpose and scope

This framework defines the future information architecture for a DUTA RANTAU Privacy Notice and Privacy Center. It records repository facts, founder policy, and factual gaps without claiming overall legal compliance.

## Proposed Privacy Center sections

### Data subjects, data categories, and collection

Potential data subjects include account holders, organization/community members, administrators, verification subjects, reporters, and people represented in moderation evidence. Repository data categories include account/profile data, organization and membership data, content, official-source data, audit metadata, verification metadata, AI operational metadata, and meeting transcript content. Collection sources are user entry, authenticated account context, organization/community activity, administrator review, official sources, and `TO_VERIFY` external processors.

### Processing purposes and sharing

Purposes include account operation, access control, organization/community participation, trusted-information delivery, safety/moderation, verification, security auditing, and optional AI/voice features. Processor and sharing details are governed by the vendor register. Hosting region, processor role, subprocessors, and cross-border status are `TO_VERIFY`.

### Retention, requests, and account lifecycle

- **RETENTION_PERIODS:** `TO_VERIFY`; no periods are established by this framework.
- **PRIVACY_REQUEST_CHANNEL:** `TO_VERIFY`; no dedicated repository mechanism was found.
- **DATA_EXPORT_WORKFLOW:** `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.
- **ACCOUNT_DELETION:** `PARTIAL / API_EXISTS`; the user-facing deletion flow and cross-system deletion evidence require review.
- **SECURITY / INCIDENT CONTACT PROCESS:** `TO_VERIFY`; do not invent a contact address.

### Consular referral boundary

`FOUNDER_POLICY: DUTA INFORM → DUTA CONNECT → OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SYSTEM EXECUTES.` DUTA may later provide curated official consular-service links, but does not initially book appointments, reserve slots, host forms, collect passport data, store appointment credentials or confirmations, or proxy-submit consular applications. Official URL registry work is `R2A-02B / NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.
