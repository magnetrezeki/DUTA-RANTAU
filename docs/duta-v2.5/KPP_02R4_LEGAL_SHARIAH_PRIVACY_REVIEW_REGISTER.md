## Shariah separation lock

`MALAYSIA_LEGAL_STATUS` and `DUTA_SHARIAH_POLICY_STATUS` remain separate.
NON_SHARIAH does not establish illegality, and illegality is not merely a
Shariah label. This register makes no fatwa or legal conclusion.
