# KPP-02R.5 Astra Handoff Register

Status: KPP-03A.1R is not started by this document. Astra receives authority,
constraints and evidence; it cannot invent features, override release gates or
change legal/governance authority.

## Required authority packet

- KPP-02R.1 master authority, supersessions and Founder Visual Anchor Lock.
- IA: Hari Ini, Tanya DUTA, Keperluan, Rantau, Saya; persistent Jaga Diri.
- Public/member boundaries; all release states from KPP-02R.4.
- Trusted-source/provenance labels: RESMI, KOMUNITI, AI GUIDANCE, USER REPORT,
  SPONSORED; paid/popular is not trusted.
- Privacy-as-UX disclosure needs; public safety is never login-gated.
- Hari Ini proactive intelligence; Tanya text plus optional voice; Info browse/
  archive layer; Jaga Diri calm elevated treatment and AI-independent fallback.
- Human-cinematic, lightweight, mobile-first, responsive/performance and
  reduced-motion constraints.
- KPP-03A.1 prototype/spec evidence under `docs/duta-v2.5/killer-product/`.

## Surface/state coverage Astra must specify

Public Landing; Hari Ini; Tanya text and optional voice; Keperluan; Kerja;
Pasar; Layanan RI; Map/Sekitar; Rantau; Kawan Rantau; Community; Organization;
Jaga Diri; Saya; Auth; DUTA Belajar where applicable; source/trust labels;
privacy explanations; loading, empty, error and degraded states.

Add Saya → Bantuan & Hubungi DUTA: Support landing, Report Problem, Contact
DUTA, Feedback, Case Status, Partnership & Investment inquiry, contextual
report entry, receipt/success and degraded-support states. The consumer-facing
prompt is “What do you need help with?”; the experience must route behind the
scenes and must not make Investor Relations visually dominant.

## Final state coverage and design limits

Astra receives authority to design normal, loading, empty, error, degraded,
blocked/unavailable, trust/provenance, public, Member, conversion, activation,
support and success/receipt states. Astra may design the approved target
experience but may not change authority, invent features, make voice mandatory,
create long-disclaimer or coercive-signup UX, or replace Founder visual anchors.

Trust data is deep; trust UX is simple: show status by default, metadata on
demand, and a freshness warning when material. `last_checked_at`,
`last_success_at`, verification method and internal audit metadata are not
required on every consumer-facing source card. Material uncertainty is never
hidden.

## Non-negotiable visual boundary

`FOUNDER_VISUAL_ANCHOR_LOCK = LOCKED`. Preserve the two existing KPP-03A.1
Founder-selected recurring subjects. Astra may not replace, regenerate, infer
biometric identity, or use face recognition. Production use needs rights/consent
and final Founder approval; the future non-biometric anchor manifest is blocked
until that approval.
