# KPP-03A Auth and Onboarding Visual Direction
A compact 440px maximum sheet/page with a contextual title such as “Simpan untuk
nanti”. State why identity is needed. Google is the first 48px button; email is
the next 48px outlined action. Preserve provider branding only using approved
assets later; no invented Google logo in the design specification.

Email reveals one labeled email field, then generic waiting message, resend
cooldown, change email and cancel. Expired link offers request-new-link; provider
failure offers the other approved method. No account enumeration or token in UI.
Returning users use the same entry; compatible legacy password is a quiet
existing-account option, not new-user primary content.

New-account 18+ attestation is unchecked, plainly worded and associated with its
label. It is required at the governed identity boundary, subject to legal review.
No full DOB, unnecessary birth year, mandatory city, profession or long form.
Name/coarse context is optional or just-in-time. Auth cancel retains public value;
successful Auth returns to the intended task. Prototype F shows the entry state.
