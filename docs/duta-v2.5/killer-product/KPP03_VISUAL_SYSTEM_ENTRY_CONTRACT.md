# KPP-03 Visual System Entry Contract

Purpose: authoritative frozen architecture inputs for KPP-03. KPP-03 may design
the visual system but may not alter product architecture for visual symmetry.

## First screens and states

Design in this order:

1. Public landing first viewport: five-second comprehension.
2. Mobile and desktop shell with five intents and persistent Jaga Diri.
3. ASK DUTA input, MENDENGAR/MEMAHAMI/MENCARI SUMBER/SIAP and failure states.
4. DUTA TODAY anonymous, new authenticated, returning, empty and degraded.
5. ESSENTIAL discovery/result/detail and CONNECT discovery/detail.
6. Unified Auth entry, magic-link waiting/expired and collision/provider errors.
7. ME control center and privacy/permission states.
8. Jaga Diri urgent/no-location/offline/fallback states.

## Frozen hierarchy

- Level 0: audience, value, first action and independent boundary.
- Level 1: TODAY / ASK DUTA / ESSENTIAL / CONNECT / ME plus persistent Jaga Diri.
- Level 2+: contextual capability, detailed action, then advanced controls.
- Google is primary Auth; email magic link fallback; value precedes login.
- Free organization presence; no paid purchase CTA or public pricing.
- Locked/deferred capabilities do not occupy primary visual space.

## Visually open

Typography, color refinement, spacing, motion, illustration, icon treatment,
component styling, elevation, responsive composition and brand expression remain
open, subject to accessibility, performance and human-cinematic goals.

## Elements that cannot be diminished

Jaga Diri access; official/community/AI status; source and checked date;
independent/non-government and executor boundaries; AI disclosure; permission
purpose and denial path; Auth benefit/cancellation; legal-review-conditioned 18+
attestation; loading/empty/error/offline/degraded states; and external official
destination clarity.

## Mobile constraints

All five intents remain labeled and reachable with thumb-accessible targets.
ASK DUTA central emphasis cannot obscure neighboring labels. Jaga Diri remains
one tap away and is not hidden behind overflow. Design for low-end viewports,
large text, keyboard/screen reader use, reduced motion, slow/offline networks and
permission denial. Voice never replaces text.

KPP-03 must demonstrate the frozen architecture across states before decorative
page expansion. It must not add routes, capabilities, provider promises, fake
personalization or unavailable commercial actions.
