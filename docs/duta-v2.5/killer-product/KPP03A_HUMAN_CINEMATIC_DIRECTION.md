# KPP-03A Human-Cinematic Direction
## Direction: Teman di Rantau
Warm paper, deep ink, purposeful red, ordinary human life and a calm editorial
rhythm. DUTA is the companion that helps a person decide their next step.

Primary scene brief: an adult pausing at a shaded Malaysian transit walkway,
checking a phone, with a companion nearby and believable everyday streetscape.
Natural late-afternoon light, eye-level framing, candid agency, no staged distress.
Nationality must be established by context/copy, never inferred from appearance.

Alternative production briefs: student arriving at a campus meeting point;
family member checking an official service before an appointment. Avoid uniforms
as shorthand for nationality/class, flag collage, robot imagery and fabricated
testimonials. One strong photograph is preferred to six stock tiles.

Photography remains a specified asset, not a delivered or licensed photograph.
Founder review approves composition/direction; final asset review must verify
consent/licensing, factual context and desktop/mobile crops. No image generation
was needed for this specification-only deliverable.

DUTA lettering uses brand red; RANTAU uses white on the deep-ink brand plaque.
On light surfaces retain that compact plaque for legibility rather than white
letters disappearing into the page. Do not recolor or distort existing logo
artwork; final lockup adaptation is a later approved design task.
