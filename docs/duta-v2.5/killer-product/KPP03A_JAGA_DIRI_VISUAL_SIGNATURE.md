# KPP-03A Jaga Diri Visual Signature
Persistent teal outlined/filled shield action with visible “Jaga Diri” label,
minimum 48px touch height. Retain contrast against paper and photography; never
place directly over a busy image. Global access remains one tap.

The page starts “Butuh bantuan?” followed by calm guidance, a distinct warning
region for immediate danger, and official-contact/manual-area actions. Teal
identifies the pathway, not severity. No sirens, pulses, emergency-authority seal
or claim that DUTA dispatches help.

No-location view is the default design specimen. Manual area selection is
prominent; “Gunakan lokasi sekali” is secondary with purpose and “Tidak sekarang”.
Source/date and jurisdiction caveat remain beside contact actions. No invented
phone number, precise coordinates or current official contact in review samples.
Offline state preserves source-stamped fallback; errors offer official discovery.
Health and uncontrolled reporting remain absent. See prototype E specification.
