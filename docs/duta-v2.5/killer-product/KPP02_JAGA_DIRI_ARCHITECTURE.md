# KPP-02 Jaga Diri Architecture

Jaga Diri is a persistent safety/trust shortcut, not a normal feature tile.

## Entry points

- One-tap shell action on mobile and desktop.
- Prominent contextual action in TODAY and ESSENTIAL.
- Safety escalation in relevant ASK DUTA responses.
- Stable direct deep link `/jaga-diri`.

## Urgent path

The first view asks what kind of help is needed and immediately offers safe,
officially bounded actions. It states that DUTA is not an emergency service or
government institution. Direct danger guidance prioritizes moving to safety,
calling appropriate local emergency help and not waiting for chat replies.

Allowed architecture includes appropriate RI mission/contact discovery,
official-service navigation and sourced situational guidance. Controlled
reporting remains unavailable until its separate private/moderator-first
contract, DPIA/legal review and operations exist. Health functionality remains
deferred; clinic/hospital discovery may appear only after a separate accepted
source/referral boundary and must not imply medical advice.

## Location and jurisdiction

Default path uses manual state/city selection and the complete official
directory. Device location is optional, explicit, action-specific and
browser-local under the current contract. Denial or unavailability returns to
manual selection without reducing access.

Geographic distance must be labeled as distance, never official jurisdiction or
competence. Wording is “appropriate official contact” with a jurisdiction
caveat. Official URL, contact channel, source and checked date remain visible.

## States

- LOADING: bundled verified fallback remains available while live data loads.
- EMPTY/ERROR: open official-channel discovery; never invent a number.
- OFFLINE: show bundled data with last-checked disclosure.
- PERMISSION_REQUIRED: explain location purpose and offer manual selection.
- AUTH_REQUIRED: not used for basic safety/contact access.
- DEGRADED_PROVIDER: direct telephone/official links remain usable.
- SUCCESS: confirms only that an action/link opened, not that help was received.

Jaga Diri must stay rapidly accessible throughout Auth, errors and other
journeys; modal/sheet layers must not trap or obscure it.
