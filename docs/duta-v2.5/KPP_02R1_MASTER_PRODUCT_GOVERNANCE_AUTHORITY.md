## Product identity, Visitor and Member

DUTA RANTAU is a Malaysia-first digital companion for Indonesians/WNI living,
working, studying, travelling or building community in Malaysia. Its promise is
TEMAN DI RANTAU: useful, trustworthy, calm value before login.

Consumer registration is free. There is no consumer RM9.90 membership/paywall.

| Term | Meaning | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor | Unauthenticated/public user | A lesser commercial tier |
| DUTA Member | Registered/authenticated free account | Verified person, seller, organization representative, partner, official source, or community-creator eligibility |
| Backend user | Technical implementation term | A separate commercial consumer tier |

Privileges are composable and entity-scoped, never one linear global role.

## Public value and free Member benefit

Public access, subject to safety/product boundaries, includes general Hari Ini,
bounded Tanya DUTA, job discovery, Pasar browsing, community/activity/
organization discovery, DUTA Map/Sekitar information, Layanan RI, official RI

RI appointment/service links and Pelindungan WNI/help/hotline are public.
Safety/help is never account-gated or dependent on AI availability.

Member authority is for persistence/member action: save, follow, join, RSVP,
permitted posting/interactions, history, alerts, profile and preferences.
Free benefits DUTA can deliver may include personalized Hari Ini, saves, follows,
alerts, community participation, RSVP, appropriate Tanya DUTA history/
personalization, DUTA Belajar, DUTA ID/Profile, appropriate badges/gamification,
and DUTA Photo/Selfie Studio.

DUTA Photo is creative/souvenir functionality, not identity verification. It has
a separate privacy/purpose contract. It is not reused by default for biometric
identification, verification, advertising profiling, unrelated AI training or
other purposes without separately justified basis, notice and required consent.

## Partner, Community and Pasar Rantau

### Partner benefits

Benefits are partner-dependent and hidden/dormant until a real approved partner
and approved offer exist:

Partner application → entity/business verification → legal/regulatory screen →
Shariah screen → trust/brand/safety screen → partner approval → offer/campaign
submission → campaign review → approved → active Member benefit → expire/close.

Partner approval and campaign approval are separate. Money cannot purchase DUTA
trust. Sponsor/partner is not seller, organization verification, official source
or editorial recommendation. Paid placement is explicit. Partners/sponsors never
receive unrestricted Member broadcast access.

### Community / Kawan Rantau

Community is free at launch. Kawan Rantau is the user-facing discovery umbrella
for Orang, Komuniti and Aktiviti. Community is digital belonging/social/activity,
not proof of registered Malaysian organization/association.

Members may join subject to applicable age/safety rules. Create/own/manage needs
COMMUNITY_CREATOR_ELIGIBLE and proportional risk-based verification. Passport,
IC, selfie or SSM are not default requirements for ordinary creation unless a
separately justified legal/risk requirement establishes them. Entity-scoped roles
are owner, admin, moderator and member; ownership transfer/recovery is required.
Community status never creates seller, organization, partner or official status.

Investment/pelaburan is prohibited as actual Community purpose/activity:
investment/trading clubs/signals, fund pooling, scheme promotion, investment
recruitment/referral and financial solicitation. Apply to behavior/purpose, not
keywords alone, and consider it across UGC. Factual financial literacy,
anti-scam education and economic information can be separately classified where
they are not recommendation, solicitation or regulated service.

### Pasar Rantau

Pasar Rantau status is CONDITIONAL_LAUNCH, DISCOVERY_AND_CONNECT_ONLY and
LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Product flow: Discover → Evaluate → Connect.

This authority does not permit DUTA checkout, wallet, escrow, settlement,
payment processing, refund adjudication or delivery execution.

Seller lifecycle: Member → Apply Seller → Application → required business/

## Kerja, Layanan RI, Jaga Diri and DUTA Belajar

### DUTA Kerja

DUTA Kerja is information, discovery and official-source navigation, not an
employment agency. It must support navigation/connection to official
KP2MI/SISKOP2MI sources subject to availability, terms, feasibility and
legal/regulatory review.

Preferred permitted pipeline: Official source → source verify → fetch/ingest →
normalize → dedup → freshness → DUTA Kerja → open official source.

If ingestion/API/fetch is unavailable/disallowed, use official-source navigation.
Do not circumvent restrictions. Tanya DUTA may support JOB_SEARCH but must never
invent job vacancies; answers need approved/trusted sources. Employer submission,
placement/matching, application execution and fees remain outside launch unless
separately approved.

### Layanan RI and Jaga Diri

Layanan RI belongs under KEPERLUAN and covers KBRI Kuala Lumpur; KJRI Johor
Bahru, Penang, Kuching and Kota Kinabalu; and KRI Tawau. It provides consular
information, official navigation and appointment/service links. Access is public.
DUTA navigates/refers; official authority/system executes. A link may be exposed
where policy permits while its verification/freshness remains honestly shown.
Unverified sources must not be labeled RESMI.

Jaga Diri is persistent elevated protection/safety, separate from ordinary
appointment navigation. It includes Pelindungan WNI, verified protection
hotline/WhatsApp navigation, legal-help navigation when verified/approved, and
official complaint/protection channels. It is public, AI-independent, and
degrades safely. DUTA is not an emergency service or government authority.

### DUTA Belajar

DUTA Belajar is secondary learning/compliance, not primary navigation. Potential
tracks: Sebelum ke Malaysia; Hidup di Malaysia; Kerja di Malaysia; safety/
anti-scam; Seller Academy; Community Creator; Organization/Admin learning.
Authority is distinguished as DUTA Learning, Official Guidance or Partner
Learning. Completion can support eligibility/acknowledgement, never government
certification without evidence and authorization.

## Shariah, moderation and integrity

Maintain separate MALAYSIA_LEGAL_STATUS and DUTA_SHARIAH_POLICY_STATUS.
NON_SHARIAH does not mean illegal; illegal does not mean merely non-Shariah.
Islamic financial matters defer to applicable Malaysian requirements and
authoritative Shariah governance/advice. DUTA AI/Codex is not final religious
authority.

Maintain a versioned Shariah Policy Registry: policy ID, category, scope, basis/
reference, effective date, version, reviewer/authority, decision and exception
rule. Qualified human Shariah review is required for final high-impact religious
classification where appropriate.

Hybrid moderation: Prevent → Detect → Hold/Allow/Block → human review where
ambiguous/high-impact → Enforce → Appeal → Audit. It covers scam/phishing, spam,
harassment, hate/incitement, threats/violence, extremism, explicit material,
child sexual exploitation, drugs, gambling, illegal goods/services, doxxing,
impersonation, high-risk misinformation and manipulation. Context matters;
keyword-only enforcement is insufficient. AI is a safety signal, not final
legal/religious judge for ambiguous/high-impact cases.

The shared Anti-Spam, Abuse & Platform Integrity Contract applies horizontally
to Member, Community, Organization, Seller, Partner/Sponsor, Pasar, Aktiviti and
other UGC. It includes semantic rate limits, progressive internal trust, bot/
automation, duplicate/link/velocity/coordinated-behavior risk, malicious-report
detection, cooldowns, restrictions, review, appeals and audit. Do not expose a
universal public trust score. Report count is not guilt. No unrestricted
Member-to-Member mass messaging; later messaging requires governed connection/
permission. Roles never grant mass-message authority.

DUTA Points are non-cash gamification, never money, stored value, investment,
tradable asset or transferable financial instrument. Reward meaningful/verified
participation, not raw volume. Badges are distinct from identity, seller,
organization and official verification.

## DUTA Control Center, enforcement and notifications

DUTA CONTROL CENTER uses scoped RBAC: Platform Super Admin, Trust & Safety,
Member Support, Seller Review, Organization Review, Partnership/Sponsor,
Official Source, Jobs, Shariah Review, Privacy/Data, AI/Knowledge, and
Broadcast/Communication. Least privilege, separation of duties and audit apply.
Admin security needs MFA/passkey direction, privileged-session controls,
high-risk re-authentication, suspicious-access detection and incident response.

Audit: who → action → entity → before → after → reason → timestamp. High-impact
operations may need dual approval.

Authorized Trust & Safety/Platform Admin may warn, restrict, suspend, ban/
terminate where policy permits, reinstate, hold/hide/remove/restore UGC,
restrict scoped entity capabilities, review history and process appeals. Automation
may proportionately rate-limit, cooldown, spam/link-block, temporarily hold/
restrict or emergency-suspend under versioned policy/risk thresholds. It is not
default final authority for permanent high-impact termination.

Significant enforcement records actor, target, action, scope, reason code, policy
ID/version, evidence, timestamp, duration, appeal eligibility and review/reversal.
Do not silently edit Member-authored content. States: held, hidden, removed,
restored. Evidence retention follows applicable policy. Scoped enforcement is
required: seller suspension/community restriction does not automatically ban a
Member. Super Admin never bypasses audit, separation, appeal or abuse-of-admin
safeguards.

DUTA Broadcast & Notification Center permits platform-wide broadcast only to
authorized roles. Channels may include push, inbox, Hari Ini, banner and
permitted email. Classes: Critical Safety, Official Service, DUTA Service,
Member Update, Community, Partner Benefit and Marketing. All-Member broadcast
requires scope preview, confirmation, audit and dual approval where policy
requires; preferences apply. Community/organization admins are entity-scoped.
Partners/sponsors have no direct platform-wide authority.

CROSS_BORDER_FLOW = NOT_CONCLUSIVELY_ESTABLISHED_FROM_REPOSITORY_EVIDENCE unless

## Tanya DUTA, search and age

Tanya DUTA supports text and optional voice with one intent → trusted retrieval
→ provenance → answer → action system. Correction path: Report answer → source
check → knowledge/human review → correction → version → propagate. A report does
not automatically overwrite knowledge.

Search/recommendation distinguishes official/trusted information, organic
relevance and sponsored placement. Paid, popular and sponsored are not trusted/
official. Sponsors cannot buy RESMI, verification, factual AI answers, hidden
preference or trust ranking. Later sponsorship requires disclosure.

AGE_CONTRACT = PROPOSED / LEGAL_CLASSIFICATION_REQUIRED.

Public information does not collect age unnecessarily. Possible under-16
public-only, 16–17 restricted Member and 18+ privileged-role models are proposed
architecture only. Seller, Community Owner/Admin, Organization Representative and

implementation. Historical blanket age assumptions have no current authority.

## Hari Ini, Tanya DUTA and trusted information

Hari Ini and Tanya DUTA are complementary, not competing, experiences.

- **HARI INI = PROACTIVE INTELLIGENCE.** DUTA proactively surfaces the
  curated, relevant information that matters to a person in Malaysia. Possible
  surfaces include Penting Hari Ini, Untuk Anda, Sekitar Anda, Update Resmi and
  Lanjutkan. Hari Ini is not a chronological social-media feed.
- **TANYA DUTA = ON-DEMAND / REACTIVE INTELLIGENCE.** The person initiates a
  need by text or optional voice. Its preferred experience is: User intent →
  Understand → Search trusted information → Answer → Action.

When a real DUTA action or trusted source is available, Tanya DUTA must not
behave as a generic chatbot. Actions may open an official RI service or
appointment source, an official job source, an applicable DUTA capability,
verified protection contact, or relevant trusted information.

Both experiences consume one Trusted Information Engine. The same engine also
feeds Info Rantau, which remains the browse/archive/full-update and
source-oriented exploration layer:

Trusted Information Engine → Hari Ini (proactive delivery) / Tanya DUTA
(on-demand retrieval and action) / Info Rantau (browse and archive).

Official updates, including KBRI/KJRI social/news information, pass: Official
Source → Fetch → Verify → Normalize → Deduplicate → Classify → Prioritize →
Publish. Hari Ini shows only the curated/relevant subset; raw social feeds are
not dumped directly into it. Full trusted browsing/archive may remain in Info
Rantau / Semua Update.

The Killer Product experience must retain: **5-second wow** (DUTA is clearly
for life in Malaysia), **30-second clarity** (the principal help is apparent),
and a **magic moment** where Tanya DUTA produces Need → Trusted answer → Useful
action. DUTA must not reduce to menus or a generic AI chatbot.

Trust identity is visible where relevant and distinguishes RESMI, KOMUNITI, AI
GUIDANCE, USER REPORT and SPONSORED. RESMI requires appropriate official-source
verification. VERIFIED is not SAFE; POPULAR and PAID are not TRUSTED; SPONSORED
is not OFFICIAL; report count is not guilt.

## Human-cinematic design, Jaga Diri and privacy-as-UX

The approved art direction is TEMAN DI RANTAU: human-cinematic, lightweight and
mobile-first. Photograph the life of people DUTA exists to help rather than
photographing DUTA features. Malaysia should appear as lived environment, not a
forced prop. Do not replace Founder-approved recurring hero visual anchors with
unrelated/random AI faces without Founder approval. Production use of
identifiable human imagery needs appropriate rights/consent.

**FOUNDER_VISUAL_ANCHOR_LOCK = LOCKED.** The two existing Founder-selected
KPP-03A.1 hero subjects are recurring DUTA visual characters, not stock-photo
models. Their facial identity and recognizable appearance must remain consistent
in subsequent prototype hero imagery. Scenes, wardrobe, framing and environment
may change naturally under the DUTA Life photography direction, but
implementation or future visual-generation work must not silently substitute
either subject for convenience, asset availability or generative variation. Any
intentional replacement requires explicit Founder approval. Production use
remains subject to appropriate image rights/consent and final asset approval.

Cinematic is not a heavy-media interpretation. Performance is a product
requirement: optimized responsive imagery, mobile-specific crops, modern image
formats, no unnecessary autoplay hero video, below-fold lazy loading, restrained
motion, reduced-motion support and minimal font payload.

Jaga Diri is a persistent, elevated safety/protection shortcut: priority
treatment, not panic treatment. It should be calm and highly discoverable (for
example warm amber and appropriate shield/heart language), not default
alarmist/SOS red. It is specifically for Pelindungan WNI/protection help, not
ordinary consular appointment navigation.

Privacy is product UX, not only a footer. When meaningful personal data is
requested, DUTA explains what is requested, why, what capability it enables and
how it is governed where appropriate. This applies especially to Member account
identity, later age assurance, location, Seller/Organization verification,
Community Creator eligibility and DUTA Photo.

## UX and continuity

Primary navigation is HARI INI / TANYA DUTA / KEPERLUAN / RANTAU / SAYA, with
persistent elevated JAGA DIRI.

- KEPERLUAN: Kerja, Pasar Rantau, Layanan RI, DUTA Map/Sekitar.
- RANTAU: Kawan Rantau (Orang, Komuniti, Aktiviti) and Organisasi.
- Info Rantau: trusted content layer feeding Hari Ini and Tanya DUTA, with
  browse/archive as needed; not necessarily primary navigation.

Critical public/safety navigation, especially Jaga Diri/Pelindungan WNI, must
work in degraded mode if AI is unavailable.
